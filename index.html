      07

      Message

      From the founding partners

      Players first. Long term. Every frame owned.

      To players, partners and the creators who trust us with their worlds:

      India has one of the largest player bases on earth, and it has yet to ship a global hit. We don't think that is a talent problem. It is a design problem, a cost problem and a patience problem. Gattabara Games exists to solve all three.

      We make decisions differently from most studios, so it is worth writing down how:

      1. We start with the player and work backwards. Feel is decided in greybox, before art and before marketing. If a mechanic doesn't feel right with boxes, it won't feel right with polish.
      2. We play the long game. We will turn down revenue that dilutes an IP, and accept short-term cost when it compounds into a better game.
      3. We spend like owners. Every rupee is either on screen or making what is on screen better. A small core, an on-call roster and no idle bench.
      4. Every frame has a name behind it. Each system, scene and line of code has one owner. Authorship creates accountability.
      5. We decide fast and correct fast. Most decisions can be reversed, so we make them with the information we have and fix them in the next build. The few that can't be reversed, we make slowly.
      6. Our games and our services feed each other. Every world we build surfaces new talent, and that talent becomes the roster our partners hire.

      We will make mistakes. When we do, we will say so, fix them and keep the bar where it is.

      Our measure is simple. When a player has one free evening, do they choose our world? Everything else is detail.

      We are early. We think that is an advantage.

      The Founding PartnersGattabara Games LLP · Bengaluru · October 2026
